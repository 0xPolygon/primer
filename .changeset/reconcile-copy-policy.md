Branches and forks follow the copy policy for every fragment: comment marks in
page text, embed and widget label fragments are re-pointed at the branch's
thread copies (branch) or stripped (fork); each widget block gets a copy of its
`widgets` row under a new id, rendered again under it, with its label, part and
data-point threads re-created on a branch; a page's stamped source is copied
into the copy's own prefix, keyed by its first checkpoint.
