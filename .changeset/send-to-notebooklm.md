Add "Send to NotebookLM…" to the document menu. One click copies the version
you're viewing to the clipboard as Markdown — title, description, a link back
to Primer, the document body with its headings, lists and tables, and
(optionally) its comment threads — and opens a short panel with an "Open
NotebookLM" button and where to paste it. From there, NotebookLM's Audio
Overview turns the document into a two-host podcast. The panel can also
download the same Markdown for NotebookLM's file upload. Everything happens in
the browser; nothing new is exposed or sent anywhere by Primer.
